About

Accelerating frontier technology.

Maiora Ventures is a venture capital firm specializing in funding the transition to an AI-powered economy. Maiora is a leading investor into critical technologies, specifically AI infrastructure and compute, robotics & leading AI application companies.

Maiora runs a multi-stage strategy, from seed to late stage, with cheque sizes ranging from $200k to $50m. At the early stage, it is one of the most active Y Combinator investors globally.
