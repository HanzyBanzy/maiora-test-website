About

Accelerating frontier technology.

Maiora Ventures is a venture capital firm specializing in funding the transition to an AI-powered global economy. We’re one of the most active investors in AI infrastructure and application companies, with a portfolio that spans the full AI stack — from silicon and compute to models and vertical software.

Our fund platform gives LPs targeted exposure to high-conviction themes including AI infrastructure, frontier compute, and tech-enabled businesses. We back exceptional founders at the earliest stages and follow our conviction through to growth.
